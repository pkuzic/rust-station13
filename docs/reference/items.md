# Simple items

`NSV_Luna.dmm` holds many small things that nothing else needs yet: stock parts, stamps, folders,
surgical tools, boxes, belts, knives, shields and so on. They are here so the decks look and fill up as
they should. All of them can be picked up, carried, put in bags, and hit with, at Luna's force. Each is
listed in `game/classic/content/items.ron`.

Luna's uses for them aren't built, for example:

- **Chemistry:** droppers and sprays hold nothing yet (`chemistry.md` has the rest).
- **Surgery:** the tools only cut as weapons.
- **Paperwork:** stamps don't stamp, and folders don't hold papers.
- **Construction:** stock parts build nothing, and the new sheets aren't usable yet.
- **Devices:** scanners and analyzers don't scan. Timers, sensors and igniters don't assemble.
- **Security:** riot shields don't block.
- **Containers:** boxes, belts and backpacks open and hold things, but start empty, apart from the
  spare handcuffs.
